METHODS & PROVENANCE

Read the evidence with its limits

  • The 2016 discovery year informed checkpoint selection. These results do not establish independent confirmation.
  • Italian XBT QF=1 corrected temperatures are converted to potential temperature with TEOS-10 using GLORYS salinity. Predictions are interpolated to native observation depths.
  • Italian evaluation uses one cruise. Confidence intervals and significance claims are withheld for insufficient independent clusters.
  • GLORYS here is the packaged reference, with approximately 0.15 °C quantization. Native floating-point GLORYS is required before a delivered-product superiority claim.
  • A single generated member cannot establish ensemble calibration. Its CRPS equals absolute error.
  • Target cells and their one-cell buffer are masked across overlapping patches. Complete cruise-level conditioning exclusion cannot be verified without cluster identities in the compact context store.
