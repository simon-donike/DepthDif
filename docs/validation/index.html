METHODS & PROVENANCE
Limitations and provenance
  • The 2016 discovery year informed checkpoint selection. These results do not establish independent confirmation.
  • Italian XBT QF=1 corrected temperatures are converted to potential temperature with TEOS-10 using GLORYS salinity. Predictions are interpolated to native observation depths.
  • Italian evaluation uses 102 casts from two missions. Two missions are too few for reliable uncertainty ranges or broad significance claims.
  • GLORYS here is the packaged reference, with approximately 0.15 °C quantization. Native floating-point GLORYS is required before a delivered-product superiority claim.
  • Single-generation CRPS equals absolute error. The Italian ensemble section evaluates sampled uncertainty separately; LPIPS and spectra compare dense fields with GLORYS, not with XBT observations.
  • Target cells are masked across overlapping patches; Italian XBT targets also have a one-cell buffer. Complete cruise-level conditioning exclusion cannot be verified without cluster identities in the compact context store.
